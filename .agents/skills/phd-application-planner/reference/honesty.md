# Verification & Accuracy Guidelines (Honesty & Grounding Protocol)

1. **Official Page Verification**:
   - Deadlines, TOEFL/GRE minimums, and application fee waivers must be sourced from official `.edu` admissions pages or verified university portals.
   - When official numbers cannot be extracted, mark status as `Live/Needs Review` or `Not Found` rather than estimating.

2. **Stipend Verification & Cost-of-Living Grounding**:
   - Base stipend amounts must reflect official department assistantship rates (TA/RA) or graduate union minimums.
   - Real stipends must use explicit regional Cost-of-Living (COL) multipliers and show both nominal and COL-adjusted figures transparently.

3. **Faculty & Grant Signals (NSF, NIH, DARPA)**:
   - Recent grant funding signals (NSF award numbers, NIH RePORTER awards, DARPA grants) must be verified via official award search portals or public lab announcements.
   - Never fabricate publication claims or grant IDs. If grant data is unverified, provide 1-click search links to `nsf.gov/awardsearch` and `reporter.nih.gov` for user verification.
