# PhD Intake Question Bank

Interactive 5-question intake bank for establishing applicant profile, subfield preferences, and constraints.

## Round 1: Target Degree & Subfield Taxonomy
- Primary research field (e.g. Computer Science, Operations Research, Applied Math, Statistics, Data Science).
- Top 3-5 subfields or research interests (e.g. Optimization, RandNLA, Machine Learning Systems, Sequential Decision Making, Inverse Problems).

## Round 2: Target Regions & Financial Minimums
- Target countries/regions (US East Coast, US West Coast, Midwest, UK, EU, Canada, Singapore).
- Minimum acceptable annual stipend amount & currency floor.

## Round 3: Faculty Advisor (PI) Preferences
- Preferred PI seniority/profile (Established Full Professors, Rising Star Tenure-Track Assistant Professors, Large Labs vs Small Labs).
- Grant funding requirements (NSF, NIH, DARPA, DOE, Industry sponsored labs).

## Round 4: Application Constraints & Standardized Tests
- GRE status (Waived, Optional, Retaking) and TOEFL/IELTS scores.
- Recommender availability & letter strategies.

## Round 5: Program Pool Size & Priority Weights
- How many total programs to target (e.g. 20-25 PhD routes + 6-10 MS backups).
- Relative weight priorities across: Research Fit, Publication Evidence, Recommender Match, Route Fit, Financial Feasibility.
